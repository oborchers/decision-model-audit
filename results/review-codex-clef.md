Comparing one model’s alternate field with other systems’ reported confidence fields changes the comparison; the report also cites an embedding classifier at 0.029. Present 0.032 explicitly as a sensitivity result, not an unqualified ranking.

4. **Medium confidence: separate P1 observation from interpretation.** `results/REPORT.md:212` accurately reports the MAEs and jump at 50%, but the claim that `noul` “behaves like a confidence that the statement leans towards yes” is a proposed mechanism, not established by these probes. 5. **High confidence: narrow the budget guarantee.** `notes/protocol.md:136` calls USD 5 a hard cap and asserts per-block caps. `src/dma/cf_client.py:51-67` checks spend before concurrent requests, allowing in-flight requests to exceed a cap; `:27` defaults the block cap to infinity, and `scripts/run_clef.sh` does not set it. Neither issue affected the observed USD 1.12 spend.
